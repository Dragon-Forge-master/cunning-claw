Cunning Claw / Get set up

Get set up on Windows, Mac or Linux.

Three steps: download and double-click, give him an AI model to think with, and approve what he asks. Version 0.5.2 is the current release, and it is free.

Step one · version 0.5.2 · free

Download and double-click.

Pick your computer and run the installer. No terminal. Your keys, settings and memory live in your own folder, so updating later is just installing the new version over the old one.

The first time you open it

The installers are not code-signed yet, so your computer will ask once whether you trust them.

  • Windows shows a blue warning. Press More info, then Run anyway. Windows has had some hours of real use and is in beta.
  • On a Mac, the first time, macOS will ask you to confirm: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway (on older versions of macOS, right-click the app and choose Open). Apple Silicon is M1 and later; Intel is older Macs. Not yet tried on a real Mac.
  • Linux, AppImage: right-click the file, Properties → Allow executing as program, then double-click it.
  • Ubuntu or Debian: take the .deb (33 MB) instead and install it with sudo apt install ./cunningclaw_0.5.2_amd64.deb.

Tried, and not yet tried

Each installer was installed and started on GitHub's own Windows, Mac and Linux machines before it was published. The Intel Mac and ARM Linux builds are the same app, not yet tried on that hardware. Day to day he runs on Linux; Windows has had some hours and is in beta; a real Mac has had none yet.

Step two

Give him something to think with.

He needs an AI model. When he first starts, your browser opens on a setup page that walks you through it.

  • Free

    A free Google key

    Any Google account, about two minutes, no card. The setup page takes you through it one step at a time.

    On Google's free plan, Google may use what you send to improve its products, and there are daily limits.

  • Free · private

    A local model

    Run a model on your own computer with Ollama and nothing leaves the machine at all.

    Slower and less clever than the hosted models.

  • Pay as you go

    Your own key

    One key from OpenRouter, or straight from Google, OpenAI or Anthropic if you already have one. Paste it into his Keys page. Your key, your rates.

    Every reply's cost is shown on screen.

Step three

Approve what he asks.

Start in Learn mode: twelve short lessons, with practice cards where nothing real will happen. You learn to read an approval card, to spot an email that tries to give orders, and what leaves your machine.

Then give him a small job. Anything that sends, spends, publishes or deletes stops and shows you a card first. How the cards work

Add your phone, if you want

Pocket Claw is his own phone app, Android first. Pair it by scanning a QR code on his screen: your phone reaches home through our end-to-end encrypted relay, or through Tailscale, your own private network. Telegram and Discord work as a second line too, and ntfy sends a push alert with no account.

Not yet tried

Pocket Claw has been driven in an emulated phone so far; no real phone has paired through the relay yet. It needs him running on your computer first.

Updating

Install the new version over the old one. Your keys, settings and memory are in your own folder and are left alone. If you switch on the update light, he fetches one small file a day from this site to see whether there is a newer version, with nothing about you in the request.

For developers

From source, or one command.

One TypeScript process on Node 22, with two runtime dependencies. It binds to your own machine only, behind a token, and your keys stay in .env.

From source

git clone https://github.com/Dragon-Forge-master/cunning-claw.git
cd cunning-claw && ./install.sh
# then
npm run dev   → http://127.0.0.1:3900

Before you ask

Setting up: straight answers.

Do I need to be technical to install Cunning Claw?

Less than you might think, and it is new. A double-click installer, then a setup page in your browser that walks you through a free Google key. A window stays open while he runs. Windows and macOS will warn you once, because the installers are not code-signed yet.

Can I run it without paying anything?

Yes. The software is free and open source, and stays free. For the AI he thinks with, start on Google's free plan with a free key, or run a local model through Ollama and pay nothing. On Google's free plan, Google may use what you send to improve its products.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me about the installer?

Because the installers are not code-signed yet. On Windows press More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. Each installer was installed and started on GitHub's own Windows, Mac and Linux machines before it was published.

Does Cunning Claw work on a Mac?

There are builds for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and each was installed and started on GitHub's Mac machines. Nobody on the project has yet run it on a real Mac, and its sandbox is untested on that hardware. If you try it, tell us what happens.

Does my computer have to be on?

Yes. He runs on your computer and works while it is on. A phone can reach him and answer his cards, but the work happens at home.

How do I update?

Install the new version over the old one. Your keys, settings and memory live in your own folder and are left alone.
