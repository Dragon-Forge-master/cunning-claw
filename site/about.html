Cunning Claw / About

Named for the Welsh cunning folk. Made in Cardiff.

Cunning Claw is a free, open-source AI agent made by Dragon Forge AI in Cardiff, Wales. It runs on your own computer, does the legwork, and waits for your yes before anything that matters.

Where the name comes from

The cunning folk were tradespeople, not mystics.

Cunning folk is a real occupational term from British history, and Wales had its own version of it: the dynion hysbys, “the knowing ones”. They worked in villages across Wales into the twentieth century.

You went to a dyn hysbys with a job: find who stole the horse, read the weather for a sailing, treat a sick child. He worked, and you paid him. The most famous were John Harries and his son Henry, of Cwrt-y-Cadno in Carmarthenshire, who practised as physicians and astrologers. Their working papers survive at the National Library of Wales: prescriptions, payment records, patient correspondence. A case file, in other words, of who asked for what and what was done.

That is the posture this software is built to. Not a companion, not a chat partner, not a personality to be enjoyed for its own sake. A practitioner you bring a job to. He does the job, and he says what he did.

A cunning man's stock in trade also included telling clients when they were being had. Which is what this one does when a web page tries to give him orders.

The longer account, with its sources

Not the game

This Cunning Claw is software. In the video game Diablo II, Cunning claws are items for the Assassin; this project has nothing to do with the game.

Who makes it

Dragon Forge AI, in Cardiff.

Cunning Claw is made by Dragon Forge AI, in Cardiff, Wales, a studio that also builds websites, apps and AI tools for businesses. It is built first for the people who run small businesses and work for themselves, starting at home in Wales, and it is free to anyone, anywhere.

The code is open source under the MIT licence: anyone can read it, run it and change it. It is one program with two outside dependencies, and 1111 automated tests at version 0.5.2.

  • Free, and staying free. The free version is never crippled, rate-limited, or feature-gated. That is in writing. A plan at £7 a month, for people who would rather not learn what a key is, is planned and not on sale yet.
  • No account, no telemetry, no tracking. Everything that leaves your machine, listed.
  • No crypto token, ever. There is no Cunning Claw token, and there never will be.

The honest bit

It is new. It has run on very few machines, and nothing here has been independently audited. What is untried is listed wherever it matters: on the set-up page and the security page.

The doctrine

Local first. Human consent where there are consequences.

Yn lleol yn gyntaf · Caniatâd dynol pan fo canlyniadauLocal first · Human consent where there are consequences

Every claw carries that line, and prints it when it starts, with three wards beneath it. Each Welsh name is a real part of the program, doing real work. Nothing is printed that is not running.
