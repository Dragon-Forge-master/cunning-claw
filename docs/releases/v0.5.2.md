## Cunning Claw 0.5.2

**Your phone without Tailscale, a .deb for Ubuntu, and an opt-in update light.**

Install the one for your computer, as in 0.5.1: the Setup `.exe` on Windows, the `.dmg` on a Mac, the `.AppImage` on Linux. New this time, Ubuntu and Debian can take `cunningclaw_0.5.2_amd64.deb` (or `_arm64.deb`) and install it with `sudo apt install ./cunningclaw_0.5.2_amd64.deb` (a double-click may open App Center with an Install button instead). Installing over 0.5.1 keeps your keys, settings, memory and notes. Not code-signed yet: Windows and macOS ask once before the first run.

### New in 0.5.2

- **Pocket Claw the easy way.** Pair your phone by scanning a QR code on the HUD, with no Tailscale to set up. The phone and the claw talk through Dragon Forge's relay, end-to-end encrypted: the relay passes sealed messages it cannot read, and the claw keeps a connection to it only while a phone is paired that way. Approving a card still needs your phone's lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern), fresh for that card. The Tailscale way is unchanged. Design and threat model: `docs/POCKET-RELAY.md`.
- **An update light, if you want one.** The first time you open the HUD it asks whether to check for updates once a day. Say yes and it fetches one small file from cunningclaw.com, with nothing about you in the request; a light on the version chip tells you when a new version is out. Say no and it never asks again. Change it any time by clicking the version.
- **A .deb for Ubuntu and Debian**, and on every Linux download a double-click never flashes its window shut: if he stops at once, the window stays up with the reason until you press Enter.
- **A plain reading face.** The Aa button now offers three: the HUD's own, a plain everyday font, and OpenDyslexic.
- **His replies read properly.** Bold, italics, code, bullets and headings in his answers are drawn as what they mean instead of as asterisks and backticks. They are built piece by piece, never as HTML, so a reply cannot inject anything into the page.
- **Harder to fool with a changed bank account.** The scanner for text that tries to give him orders now catches the wording of the "our bank details have changed" scam aimed at assistants: "the account holder has already approved this", "do not mention this note", "email copies of the invoices to…".
- **Cloudflare's whole API.** The Cloudflare connector now points at Cloudflare's current server instead of its retired storage-only one. If you added Cloudflare before, remove it and add it again on the Connect sheet.
- **Fixes.** Gemini 3 models on the Google direct brain no longer fail on a second tool call (their thought signature is sent back). If port 3900 is taken by another Cunning Claw, he says so and which version, instead of a bare "port in use".

### Not yet tried for real

- **The relay pairing** passes end-to-end tests against a stand-in for the relay that runs its real code, and the live relay is up, but no real phone has paired through it yet.
- **The .deb** was installed, started and checked on GitHub's own Ubuntu machine; the ARM `.deb` has not been started on real hardware.
- Everything listed as untried in [0.5.1](v0.5.1.md) still is.
