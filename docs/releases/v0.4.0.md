## Cunning Claw 0.4.0

*Never published: everything below shipped in [0.5.0](v0.5.0.md), the first release after 0.3.0.*

Download the one for your computer, unzip it, and read **READ ME FIRST.txt**. Not code-signed yet: Windows and macOS ask once before the first run, and the read-me says what to click.

### New since 0.3.0

- **A desk marketer.** Follow-ups after a job, review requests, reminders and newsletters in your business's voice, sent through a proper mailing service (Klaviyo, MailerLite, HubSpot or Resend) after one approval per campaign. A consent list on the Desk decides who may be emailed, and a check in the code removes anyone not on it, refuses a campaign with no unsubscribe line, and never touches one-to-one email. Not legal advice: check the ICO's current guidance.
- **The house, properly** (through Home Assistant). Lights to any brightness or colour, heating to a temperature, blinds, scenes. Doorbell, motion and door alerts to your phone with quiet hours, and a visitor log that stays on your machine: events, not faces. Unlocking, disarming, or opening a garage or gate always asks you afresh, and is refused while he has just read something suspicious.
- **Projects.** Research where every claim is sourced; grant and tender drafts written against the funder's or buyer's own scoring, with gaps marked rather than invented; presentations and project plans. A tender watch for Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, and Sell2Wales through the browser. You do the submitting.
- **PDFs.** `export_pdf` turns a Desk document or a slide deck into a PDF with his own Chrome, scripts off and nothing local framed in.
- **Resend** joins the connectors (95 in all).
- **Fixes:** a Home Assistant call can no longer swap the device named on its approval card; a newsletter's unsubscribe footer is no longer cut from the preview.
