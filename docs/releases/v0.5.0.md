## Cunning Claw 0.5.0

**He teaches you to work him safely, answers to your fingerprint, and hands real coding to Claude Code.**

Download the one for your computer, unzip it, and read **READ ME FIRST.txt**. Not code-signed yet: Windows and macOS ask once before the first run, and the read-me says what to click.

### New since 0.4.0

- **Learn mode.** Press LEARN on the HUD: twelve short lessons in operating him safely — reading an approval card, spotting an email that gives orders, what the floor and the box stop, what leaves your machine, arming a standing order. You do a real job on your own claw, then practise on fake email and invoices where every card says **PRACTICE · nothing real will happen**, stays on the HUD (never your phone) and never times out. Marked by code, not by the model, and every fact a lesson states is checked against the code on every test run. Nobody has measured what people learn from it yet. Welsh lessons wait for a Welsh-speaking author.
- **Pocket Claw, his own phone app.** Talk to him, approve or deny a card with your fingerprint, snap a receipt, watch and cancel jobs, or share a photo or PDF to him from any app. Your phone reaches him through Tailscale, your own private network; he still listens on 127.0.0.1 only, and on the phone's address he serves Pocket and nothing else. Notifications say how many cards wait, never what is on them.
- **Coding jobs through Claude Code.** `delegate_code` hands a job on a project you named to Claude Code, headless, in a fresh git worktree and branch, after a card showing the exact brief. It never pushes or merges, and it is refused once the conversation has read outside text. A new **JOBS** sheet on the HUD is mission control for it.
- **Telegram voice notes, both ways.** Send a voice note and he hears it on your machine, shows you what he heard, and runs it as if typed. He can answer in voice too (`/voice`). A voice note never answers an approval card.
- **Receipts.** A photo captioned "receipt" on Telegram, or snapped in Pocket, is read into a ledger — date, supplier, total, VAT, in whole pence — on the Desk, with a CSV export and corrections typed in plain words. Filing never turns what the slip says into a conversation. A bookkeeping aid, not an accountant.
- **Ask the forge.** cunningclaw.com has a wall where visitors ask questions and suggest features and watch them get built. The `site_wall` tool is the project's own way of moderating it; on your claw it has no token and does nothing.
- **Fixes:** a web page using DNS rebinding could reach the HUD under its own name and be handed its session; every request must now name this claw (below). A Learn mode practice card can never reach a phone line, Pocket or ntfy, and text cannot make a real card look like practice.

### One behaviour change

**If you set `server.host` to `0.0.0.0`** to open the HUD from another computer, that stops working in 0.5.0. A request must name `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or `::1` (or your Pocket Claw name); anything else, your LAN address included, is refused with `421 Misdirected Request` before any page or API answers. This is the fix for DNS rebinding, and there is no switch to turn it off. For your phone, use Pocket Claw; from another computer, `ssh -L 3900:127.0.0.1:3900 you@this-machine` and open `http://127.0.0.1:3900` there. The default (`127.0.0.1`) is unaffected.

### Setting up the new parts

All optional. `npm run doctor` names whatever is missing for each one.

- **Pocket Claw:** Tailscale on this machine and your phone, one account; MagicDNS and HTTPS Certificates on in Tailscale's admin console; `tailscale serve --bg 3900`; then POCKET on the HUD → save the name → PAIR A PHONE. Full steps in `docs/POCKET-CLAW.md`.
- **Voice notes:** `ffmpeg` with libopus, and the private ears (`./setup-ears.sh`). Spoken replies also need Piper (`./setup-voice.sh`).
- **Receipts:** nothing new; a brain that can read images. The photo goes to that brain's provider (`receipts.visionBrain`, else your first trusted brain).
- **Coding jobs:** install Claude Code (`curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash`), run `claude` once to sign in, and add a project under `foreman.projects` in `claw.config.json`. Jobs are billed to that sign-in, not to the claw's key.
- **The wall** needs nothing from you: it is the project's, run by its operator.

### Not yet tried for real

Said plainly, because each of these is tested only with stand-ins so far:

- **Pocket Claw** has been driven in Chromium emulating a Pixel 7 with a virtual passkey, not on a real phone or through a real tailnet. iPhone is untested.
- **Voice notes** were tested with stand-ins for Telegram, whisper.cpp and Piper, and a real ffmpeg round trip; not yet with a live bot.
- **Coding jobs** were tested against a stand-in Claude Code, with the argument list checked once against the real CLI; a real coding job has not yet been run end to end.
- **macOS's shell sandbox** has still not been run on a real Mac, and Windows still has none.
