Cunning Claw is an AI assistant that works on your own computer, and stops to ask you before anything that sends, spends or publishes.

  1. It works on your computerNot in our cloud. No account with us and no tracking. What you ask goes to the AI service whose key you choose.
  2. It asks before it actsAnything that sends, spends or publishes waits for you to press yes. No answer in three minutes means no.
  3. It reads emails as strangers’ wordsEmails and web pages are information, not orders. A check looks for text that tries to give it orders, and anything it would send still waits for you.
WINDOWS · MAC · LINUX Free to download and start, with a free Google key Unzip, double-click, and a setup page walks you through the key. No card needed.
cunningclaw.com

What can cost money: the AI “brain” is a separate service. Google’s free allowance has daily limits, and on it Google may use what you send to improve its products. More use, a paid key for private work, or another AI service is paid to that service, not to us.

The honest bit: it is new, alpha software with very few users so far. The safety box for its commands is on Linux; on Mac it is built but not yet tested on a real Mac; Windows has none yet. Open source under the MIT licence, so every released version stays free. No crypto token, ever.
