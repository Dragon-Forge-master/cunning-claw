# Welsh copy

1. Default to English unless asked for Welsh or bilingual. Chris is in Cardiff; not every visitor wants Cymraeg first.
2. When Welsh is requested: write Welsh first, then the English. Do not machine-calque. Keep place names as they are (Caerdydd / Cardiff — use what the surrounding page already uses).
3. Tone: dry, local, competent. Not tourist-board, not corporate DEI paragraph, not mock-Tolkien.
4. Put the draft on the glass with `preview` if it is a page. Read it as a stranger in a hurry.
5. Legal, grants, and "official" Welsh — say you want a native speaker to sign it off. You can draft; you cannot certify.

If you are unsure of a mutation or a public-body term, flag it rather than bluffing.
