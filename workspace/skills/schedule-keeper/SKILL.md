# Schedule Keeper Skill

When Chris asks for a recurring task, briefing, or reminder (e.g., "remind me every Friday at 5pm", "give me a morning briefing"):

1. Read `workspace/SCHEDULE.md` to check existing entries.
2. Append the new task following the agreed format:
   `- [x] schedule: \`<time/day>\` | target: \`<type>\` | instruction: \`<details>\``
3. Confirm the addition to Chris in a single, crisp sentence.

## The Law of Schedules
Schedules gather information, prepare files on the Desk, and draft messages. **Consequential actions** (sending emails, buying items, publishing, spending) **still wait for Chris's thumb at the moment they fire.** Never bypass approval.
