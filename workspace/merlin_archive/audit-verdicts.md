## Standing rules distilled from the whole exchange

1. External AI output is testimony under attribution, never ground truth.
2. Self-assigned confidence tags are rhetoric, not verification — check the
   one claim you can take to a primary source.
3. Watch the premises of humble statements: a refusal can assert a myth as
   the ground it stands on.
4. Eloquence is not evidence; a concession that adds no new claims is the
   best evidence of good faith yet observed.
