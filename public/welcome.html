1 · GIVE HIM A BRAIN

The brain is the AI model he thinks with. Pick one — you can add others later from the KEYS page. Not sure? Take the free Google one.

Google Gemini FREE · NO CARD NEEDED

The easiest start. Any Google account (your Gmail) gets a free key in about two minutes:
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tick to accept Google's terms if it asks. Google sets up a project and a key for you. If no key is listed, press Create API key.
  3. Press the copy button beside the key (a long string of letters and numbers).
  4. Paste it in the box below and press CHECK & SAVE. He checks it with Google before keeping it.
Good to know: the free plan has a daily limit — if he says it is used up, it comes back at 8 am UK time. On the free plan Google may use what you send to improve its products; for private work, turn on billing in AI Studio, or use the offline brain.
